❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I translate a message on Discord?
Simply react to any message with a country flag emoji (e.g., 🇺🇸 for English, 🇰🇷 for Korean) and AirTranslator will instantly provide a translation. You can also use /quicksetup for automatic channel translation.
Is AirTranslator free to use?
Yes! AirTranslator offers a generous free tier. You can also earn bonus messages daily by voting for the bot on Top.gg.
Does it support image translation (OCR)?
Absolutely. AirTranslator uses advanced AI to detect and translate text within images. Just react with a flag to an image or use the context menu.
What makes AirTranslator better than other bots?
Unlike basic translation bots, AirTranslator uses state-of-the-art AI models that understand context, slang, and emotion, providing human-like fluency instead of literal word-for-word translations.
How do I use the Dashboard to manage my server?
Open the Dashboard from the "Official Website" link, sign in with your Discord account, then configure server settings, language channels, and view usage analytics. Changes apply instantly to connected servers and give you centralized control over translations and setups.
What does Premium include and how does billing work?
Premium unlocks unlimited translations, Personal Buddy mode, priority processing, and exclusive features. Billing is handled through the payment provider listed on the Premium page (such as Patreon) with recurring monthly payments; you can manage or cancel your subscription from that provider's account settings.
